# Privacy Policy

Big Calendar is designed to respect your privacy.

## What we collect

Nothing. Big Calendar does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any
personal information.

## Calendar access

Big Calendar asks for access to your calendars so it can display your events.
This information:

- is read on your device only,
- is used solely to show your events inside the app,
- is **never** sent to us, and
- is **never** shared with any third party.

We have no servers and no analytics. Your data stays on your iPhone.

## Purchases

The optional one-time Pro purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App
Store. We do not receive or store any payment information.
